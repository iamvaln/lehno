## 4. What the Service does

Lehno helps its users remember the dates that matter and what they know about the
people they love, and be ready when the day comes. It lets you:

- keep one page per person and note what you learn over the year;
- record dated occasions and be reminded of them;
- have someone fill in a page themselves, through a link;
- publish a Wall where the people around you find your wishes and can leave you a
  note;
- obtain, in exchange for credits, a portrait, gift ideas or a message written
  from what you noted.

The Service is provided "as is" and its features may change. It sends nothing on
your behalf: a message it produces is handed back to you so that you adjust it,
sign it and send it from your own inbox.

**Your Wall** is public only if you publish it, and shows nothing but what you

**Birthday notes** left for you are for you alone. They are never displayed
publicly, and the Service keeps no guest book.

**A reservation** on a wish is never shown together with the name of whoever made
it, unless that person chose to be known.
